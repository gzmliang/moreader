MoReader is a clean, privacy-first EPUB and PDF reader for Chrome with built-in text to speech. Open a book and read or listen to it in a comfortable, fully customizable page, with sentence-level audio sync, bilingual layouts and chapter study tools. No account, no ads, no analytics.

READING
- EPUB books, reflowable with full typography control: font family, size, line spacing, margins and letter spacing.
- PDF documents, in their original layout.
- Phonetic annotations: Pinyin and Furigana ruby text stay aligned above the characters.
- Four themes: light, sepia, eye-care green and dark.
- Works on Chrome and ChromeOS, including Chromebooks.

PDF
- Open PDFs in their original layout with zoom, fit-to-page and smooth scrolling.
- Image-based and scanned PDFs render correctly, including textbooks and older documents.
- Select any text to hear it read aloud or translate it.
- Convert a PDF into a clean, reflowable EPUB for comfortable reading and listening.

READ ALOUD
- Dual-layer sync: the current paragraph is highlighted while the sentence being spoken lights up.
- Turn any EPUB or PDF into an audiobook you can follow word by word.
- Choose browser voices for offline TTS, or natural neural cloud voices.
- Speech speed from 0.5x to 2.0x.
- Ruby annotations, brackets and citation markers are cleaned before they are spoken.
- Click any sentence to start listening from exactly there.

STUDY TOOLS
- Chapter summaries at the depth you choose.
- Bilingual reading: side by side, original only, or translation only.
- Character relationship maps and plot timelines.
- Chapter review questions with explanations.
- Export notes and summaries as EPUB or PDF.
- Generated content is cached locally, so it opens instantly next time.

SYNC (OPTIONAL)
- Synchronize reading progress, bookmarks and highlights over WebDAV: Nextcloud, Nutstore or your own server.
- Requests go only to the servers you configure, and always over HTTPS.

YOUR BOOKS STAY YOURS
- Library, reading progress, bookmarks and notes are stored in your browser. No tracking, no analytics.

Note: TTS narration uses your browser's built-in speech engine by default and works offline. A cloud narration endpoint can be configured in settings.

Install MoReader for a focused, comfortable reading and listening experience, for both EPUB and PDF.
